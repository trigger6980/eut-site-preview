Meet the five assistants

Every assistant can do every job; you choose what each one handles. Actions land in Activity history so you always know who did what.

N

Nova · Front Desk

Warm receptionist. Answers every call and text, handles common questions, captures name and contact, offers to book. Default inbound assistant.

A

Atlas · Follow-ups

Friendly follow-up specialist. Checks back with new customers quickly so quotes and bookings don’t go cold.

L

Ledger · Billing & Pay

Courteous billing help. Sends invoices and payment links, polite reminders, keeps books tidy. Never gives tax advice.

E

Echo · Social Posts

Upbeat marketer. Writes short social posts and emails with a clear call to action.

S

Sage · Schedule & To-dos

Calm all-rounder. Keeps the schedule running, sends reminders and quick texts, moves to-dos forward.

What the app covers

Ask → preview → confirm

Type “send Jane an invoice for $200” or “book Marcus Tuesday at 2.” See exactly what will happen. Confirm once — or cancel.

Website chat bubble

One script line adds Chat / Text us / Book to B12, Wix, WordPress, Squarespace, Shopify, and more.

Local AI by default

Models can run on the shop’s own computer (Ollama / LM Studio). Conversations stay on that machine. Rules still work if no model is running.

Get paid
